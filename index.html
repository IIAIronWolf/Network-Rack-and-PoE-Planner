PoE capacity overview

Red: denied port or ≥98% utilized. Amber: ≥80%, PSU fault, or port fault. Green: below 80%. Gray: budget unknown. Ratings are based on reported PoE allocation, not live AC draw. An empty PSU slot inferred from inventory is not a verified electrical status.

Upgrade review

Candidates are switches at ≥80% or with power-denied ports and fewer than two inventoried PSUs. Verify platform support, power mode, UPS/PDU capacity, and exact PSU compatibility before ordering. No projected capacity is assumed.
